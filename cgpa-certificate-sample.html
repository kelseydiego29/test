Basis of calculation

Method. Each reported syllabus grade is converted to grade points using the key opposite. The cumulative grade point average is the arithmetic mean of the grade points of all eleven reported syllabuses, carried to two decimal places and not rounded up at any intermediate step.

Unweighted CGPA — 2.64 / 4.00. All syllabuses carry equal weight (29.00 ÷ 11).

Credit-weighted alternative — 2.47 / 4.00. Where an institution weights AS Level at two credits against one credit for IGCSE, the result is 37.00 ÷ 15.

Ten-point scale. The unweighted result expressed on a 10.00 scale is 6.59 / 10.00 (credit-weighted: 6.17 / 10.00).
