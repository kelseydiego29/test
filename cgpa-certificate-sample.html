Method and scope

Method. Each reported syllabus grade is converted to grade points using the key opposite. The cumulative grade point average is the arithmetic mean of the grade points of all eleven reported syllabuses, carried to two decimal places, with no intermediate rounding.

Unweighted CGPA — 2.64 / 4.00. All eleven syllabuses carry equal weight (29.00 ÷ 11). This is the figure certified overleaf.

Credit-weighted alternative — 2.47 / 4.00. Where an institution weights AS Level at two credits against one credit for IGCSE, the result is 37.00 ÷ 15.

Ten-point scale. The unweighted result expressed on a 10.00 scale is 6.59 / 10.00; the credit-weighted result is 6.17 / 10.00.

Percentage uniform marks. The PUM shown against each syllabus is the mark reported by Cambridge on the corresponding Statement of Results. It is a position-within-grade indicator, not a raw examination mark, and is reproduced here for reference only; it does not enter the grade point calculation. The aggregate PUM across the eleven syllabuses is 69.55%.

Scope. Cambridge International does not itself issue grade point averages. This conversion is prepared by the issuing institution named overleaf, on the scale stated above. Receiving institutions that publish a different conversion scale should apply their own.
